How It Works

1. Chat Daily: Talk to your AI companion about your day, thoughts, feelings, and experiences throughout the day.

2. Automatic Journaling: Every night at midnight in your timezone, your conversations are analyzed and transformed into a reflective journal entry.

3. Weekly Insights: Every 7 days, receive personalized highlights summarizing your week and focus tasks to help you grow.

4. Reflect & Grow: Search, read, and learn from your journal entries over time to understand your emotional patterns and personal growth journey.

Technical Highlights

Built with React Native and Expo for smooth performance, LumenLeaf Journal features a cloud-based backend for reliability and scalability, real-time synchronization across devices, optimized data usage, and offline reading support. The app requires Android 5.0 (Lollipop) or higher and an active internet connection for AI features.

Perfect For

LumenLeaf Journal is ideal for anyone looking to maintain a daily journaling practice, people who find traditional journaling time-consuming, those who want to understand their emotional patterns, anyone seeking a private space for self-reflection, and individuals working on personal growth and mindfulness.
